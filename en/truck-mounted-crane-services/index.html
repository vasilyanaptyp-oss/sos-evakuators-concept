01 / Lifting and transport

One vehicle. Several jobs.

A truck-mounted crane combines goods transport with lifting and loading. We can deliver building materials to your site, transport a sauna cabin or load a damaged vehicle. Before the job, we agree the load dimensions and weight, access requirements and unloading position.

  • 01

    Building material transport

    Loading, delivery and unloading at your site. Tell us which materials you need to move and how much.

  • 02

    Crane and lifting work

    Lifting and positioning loads. We confirm suitability based on weight, reach and site access.

  • 03

    Vehicle transport for disposal

    Loading damaged or end-of-life vehicles and transporting them to an agreed collection facility.

  • 04

    Sauna cabin transport

    Lifting, loading and transporting sauna cabins and similar loads by prior arrangement.
